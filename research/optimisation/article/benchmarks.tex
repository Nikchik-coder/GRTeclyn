\section{Exact static benchmarks}
\label{app:static}

The static massless solution supplies analytical checks of the volume,
local and null-ray measures. Define $a=-\gamma/(2\pi)$ and
$y=\arctan(\eta/\eta_0)$. In the convention of Eq.~\eqref{eq:metric},
$B=1$, $\nu=0$, $\omega=0$, and
\begin{equation}
f=-2a(\pi/2-y),\qquad
(\partial_\eta\psi)^2=\frac{\eta_0^2(1+a^2)}{h^2}.
\label{eq:static-fields}
\end{equation}
In particular, $f(-\infty)=\gamma$, as required. Minimizing the sphere
radius $e^{-f/2}\sqrt h$ gives
\begin{equation}
\eta_{\rm th}=a\eta_0,\qquad
R_e=\eta_0\sqrt{1+a^2}\,e^{a(\pi/2-\arctan a)}.
\label{eq:static-throat}
\end{equation}
The angular integral in Eq.~\eqref{eq:X} cancels the $4\pi$ in the
radial NEC deficit. Changing variables to $y$ leaves
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
X&=\frac{\eta_0(1+a^2)}{R_e}
       \int_{-\pi/2}^{\pi/2} e^{a(\pi/2-y)}\,dy\\
 &=\frac{2\sqrt{1+a^2}}{a}\,e^{a\arctan a}\sinh(\pi a/2).
\end{split}
\label{eq:static-X}
\end{equation}
The continuous $a\to0$ limit gives the symmetric result
\begin{equation}
X_{\mathrm{S0},V=0}=\frac{1}{\eta_0}
 \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}\frac{\eta_0^2}{\eta^2+\eta_0^2}\,d\eta=\pi.
\label{eq:ellis-X}
\end{equation}
For D0, $a=1/2$, so
\begin{equation}
X_{\mathrm{D0},V=0}=\sqrt5\,e^{\frac12\arctan(1/2)}
 \left(e^{\pi/4}-e^{-\pi/4}\right).
\label{eq:drainhole-X}
\end{equation}
These give $\clmAnalyticSzeroX$ and $\clmAnalyticDzeroX$, respectively,
agreeing with the massless entries of Table~\ref{tab:X}.

For D0 the local radial deficit is
$|\rho+p_\eta|=e^f(\partial_\eta\psi)^2/(4\pi)$. Its derivative vanishes
at $\eta=\eta_0/4$, giving
\begin{equation}
R_e^2\max|\rho+p_\eta|=
 \frac{1}{4\pi}\left(\frac{20}{17}\right)^2
 e^{\arctan(1/4)-\arctan(1/2)}.
\label{eq:static-peak}
\end{equation}
For a radial null geodesic with Killing energy $E=1$ at positive infinity,
$\dot t=e^{-f}$ and $|\dot\eta|=1$, where a dot denotes an affine derivative.
Consequently the complete ray integral is
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
R_e\int|T_{\mu\nu}k^\mu k^\nu|\,d\lambda
 &=\frac{R_e}{4\pi}\int(\partial_\eta\psi)^2\,d\eta\\
 &=\frac{5\sqrt5}{64}
   e^{\pi/4-\frac12\arctan(1/2)}.
\end{split}
\label{eq:static-ray}
\end{equation}
Equations~\eqref{eq:static-peak} and \eqref{eq:static-ray} yield
$\clmAnalyticDzeroPeak$ and $\clmAnalyticDzeroRay$. Both reproduce the D0
reference entries of Table~\ref{tab:physics} at its displayed precision.
The different peak and ray normalizations are thus checked independently
of the potential-bearing solutions.
