To the Editors, \emph{General Relativity and Gravitation}

Dear Editors,

Please consider our manuscript \emph{``Binary Ellis--Bronnikov wormholes in 3+1 numerical relativity: mergers, fly-bys and gravitational waves''}, by Nikita M.\ Shirokov and Ilya Nachevsky, as a Research Article in \emph{General Relativity and Gravitation}.

The manuscript studies pairs of massive Ellis--Bronnikov wormholes supported by a phantom scalar field in three-dimensional numerical relativity. It examines how the signs of the scalar charges determine attraction or repulsion, how a common horizon forms around two throats in a collision, and how the instability of each throat affects mergers and fly-bys. It also reports the gravitational and scalar radiation from these encounters. The work addresses the nonlinear dynamics of exotic compact objects and their gravitational-wave signatures.

Our earlier submission to this journal was withdrawn on 21 October 2026. We now submit the revised manuscript corresponding to arXiv:2610.13206v2, with journal submission details and a disclosure of AI-assisted scientific software development added. The revision reorganises the presentation, defines the notation, and presents convergence tests using rescaled differences.

The manuscript is not under consideration by another journal. The preprint is available at \url{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13206}. The code, parameter files, diagnostic data and analysis scripts are archived at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23257999}. Funding, competing-interest, data-availability and code-availability statements are included in the manuscript.
