## 7. What this settles and what it does not

- The mode is physics: same rate at two resolutions to 9 %, moving toward the predicted band.
- The fate is not: the sign of the truncation seed flipped between the levels. No collapse or
  inflation observed without a declared seed may be quoted as the physics of the throat.
- The collapse branch ends in a marginally trapped surface losing mass (1.62 → 1.23 over 40
  units); whether it settles, and at what fraction of m, is open.
- The inflation branch deforms the compactified inner sheet by t = 100; whether that is the
  physics of the branch or the origin failing is open (a −ε arm at level 3 answers it).
- The late constraint growth on both branches is unexplained.
