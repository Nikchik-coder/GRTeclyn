## Reading

Throats read at t = 30: 6 early, 4 same, 42 late, 22 too short, 24 restart arms (own clock only).

- *early* = the companion has moved that throat's origin clock forward relative
  to an isolated throat: the environment is selecting (accelerating) the
  contraction branch there.
- *same* = neutral: the throat is on the isolated schedule until the plunge and
  V1's natural run cannot expect the environment to do the collapsing for it.
- *late / never* = the companion is holding the throat open longer than it would
  hold itself, which on the like-signed (repelling) arms is the expected sign of
  the inflation branch and should be checked against their areal radius.
