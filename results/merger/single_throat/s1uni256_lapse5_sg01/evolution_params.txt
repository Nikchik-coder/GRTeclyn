# Diagnostics.

# READ binary_throat_diagnostics.dat WITH CARE on a single wormhole.  Its
# chi*_min column is the GLOBAL minimum of chi, and for a wormhole that is NOT

# Stage 1.3 question.  The module's two-throat columns are also degenerate here
